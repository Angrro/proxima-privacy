Privacy Policy · Politica de Confidențialitate

Last updated: 5 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Proxima ("we", "us", the "App") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application. Proxima is a location-based social networking app that helps you discover and connect with people nearby. By using Proxima, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Proxima is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Email address — used exclusively to authenticate you via a passwordless "magic link". We do not use passwords.
  • Location (GPS) — your approximate geographic position is used to find and rank other users near you. Location is only accessed while you use the app.
  • Profile photos — images you upload to build your profile.
  • Profile information — data you choose to provide (such as display name, age, and any details you add).
  • Chat messages — the content of messages you exchange with other users.
  • Technical data — device push-notification token and basic technical identifiers needed to operate the service.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure (email magic link).
  • To show you nearby users and enable proximity-based matching (GPS location).
  • To display your profile and photos to other users.
  • To deliver messages between matched users.
  • To send push notifications about matches and messages.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process your data based on: the performance of a contract (providing the App's core features), your consent (for location access and push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings), and our legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and functional).

5. Third-party services

We share the minimum necessary data with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Cloudinary — stores and delivers your profile photos.
  • Render — hosts our backend servers and database.
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Email delivery provider — sends authentication magic-link emails.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

6. What other users can see

Other users of Proxima can see the information that makes up your public profile: your profile photos, the details you add to your profile, and your approximate proximity (distance), so they can decide whether to connect. Users you match with can exchange chat messages with you. Your exact GPS coordinates and your email address are never shown to other users.

7. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data (profile, photos, and messages) within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain information by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (and in particular under GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. disable location or notifications in your device settings).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.

9. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Age requirement

Proxima is intended only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.
